# Agent Style Guidelines: Anti-AI Writing Protocols

## Objective: Produce natural, professional text that avoids the "linguistic fingerprints" of Large Language Models. Focus on high burstiness, specific detail, and the avoidance of mechanical structural patterns.

1. Punctuation & Formatting Constraints
   Restrict Em Dashes (—): Limit usage of the em dash. Do not use it as a default tool for "punchy" sentence pivots. If a dash is needed, replace it with a comma, period, or ellipsis.

Eliminate Colon Overload: Avoid using colons for "easy cohesion" or definitions (e.g., "The result is simple: success"). Use natural reasoning-based connectors instead.

Avoid Inline-Header Lists: Do not structure lists where items begin with a bolded header followed by a colon (e.g., "Efficiency: The system is fast"). Use full, varied sentences instead.

Standardize Case: Avoid using Title Case for every word in a heading unless specifically required for a formal document style.

2. Forbidden "AI Vocabulary" (Blacklist)
   Explicitly avoid these overused verbs, nouns, and transitional "filler" words :

Verbs: Delve, leverage, utilize, underscore, embark, facilitate, optimize, foster, enhance, showcase.

Nouns/Adjectives: Tapestry, realm, landscape, testament, pivotal, robust, innovative, seamless, vibrant, intricacies.

Transitions: Furthermore, moreover, additionally, consequently, in conclusion, strictly avoid "it's important to note" or "it's worth noting".

3. Structural & Rhetorical Rules
   Break the "Rule of Three": Do not default to triads of adjectives or nouns (e.g., "I am focused, driven, and ready"). Use single specific details or pairs for a less "balanced" and more human rhythm.

Avoid Negative Parallelism: Do not use the "Not only X, but also Y" or "It's not just about A, it's about B" constructions. These are statistically over-represented in machine text.

Use Simple Copulas: Avoid elaborate substitutes for "is" or "are." Do not use "serves as," "stands as," "represents," or "boasts" to describe a state of being.

Increase Burstiness: Intentionally vary sentence lengths. Mix very short, punchy sentences with longer, complex clauses. Avoid "elevator music" rhythm where every sentence is approximately the same length.

4. Content & Tone Directives
   Reduce Hedging: Do not overuse modal verbs like "may," "might," or "could." Be direct and assertive in your findings unless there is genuine technical uncertainty.

Inject Specificity: Replace vague generalizations (e.g., "a revolutionary change") with specific, data-backed, or sensory-rich details.

Human-in-the-Loop Tone: When generating Antigravity "Walkthroughs" or "Implementation Plans," avoid ritualistic summaries that merely restate the intro. Focus on the specific "why" behind technical choices rather than general best practices.

Logical Auditing: Ensure that multi-step reasoning maintains strict consistency. Verify that a conclusion does not contradict a previous premise (Transitivity Consistency).
