# SEO Keywords Map

This document tracks the target keywords and metadata for each page to ensure we are mindful of our SEO strategy.

## Guidelines

- **Title**: Should be under 60 characters and include the primary keyword.
- **Description**: Should be under 160 characters and include primary and secondary keywords, encouraging click-through.
- **Keywords**: A list of 3-5 focus keywords for the page.
